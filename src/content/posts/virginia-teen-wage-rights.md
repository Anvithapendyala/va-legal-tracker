## Status & Jurisdiction

Virginia and federal wage laws apply to many hourly and youth workers. Coverage, exemptions, minimum-wage rates, and remedies depend on the job and current law.

## The Legal & Financial Rule

Under Virginia law and the federal Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), covered employers generally must pay workers for all compensable time worked and at least the applicable minimum wage, unless a specific exemption applies.

- **Off-the-clock work:** Required job duties performed before clocking in or after clocking out may count as compensable work time.
- **Virginia wage-payment protections:** The Virginia Wage Payment Act, Va. Code § 40.1-29, governs timely payment of wages and provides remedies for certain violations.
- **Breaks for younger workers:** Va. Code § 40.1-80.1 restricts work hours for employees under age 16, including a rest-period requirement after five consecutive hours of work.

## Why It Matters to Your Rights & Wallet

Compare schedules, time records, and pay stubs. If hours or wages appear missing, keep copies and consider contacting the Virginia Department of Labor and Industry or a qualified legal-aid provider. The correct process and available remedies depend on the facts.
