## Status & Jurisdiction

Virginia is within the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit. In _United States v. Simons_, 206 F.3d 392 (4th Cir. 2000), the court considered an employee's privacy expectation in a workplace computer system. The case is useful context for digital privacy, but it did not decide a student's rights in a school-issued laptop or phone.

## What Happened / The Legal Rule

In _Simons_, a federal employee used a government computer to access and download images from the internet despite an agency policy allowing monitoring and inspection. The Fourth Circuit concluded that, in the circumstances and under that policy, he had no reasonable expectation of privacy in the files found on the system. The decision turns on the system, the user's notice, and the employer's access; it is not a blanket rule that every government-owned device may be searched for any reason.

Public-school searches have a different controlling Supreme Court framework. Under _New Jersey v. T.L.O._, a school search generally must be justified at its start and reasonable in scope. A school technology policy, what the student was told, the purpose of the search, and the information sought can all matter. _Riley v. California_ recognized the extensive privacy interests in modern phones in the arrest-search context, but it did not establish a categorical rule for school-issued devices.

Read [_United States v. Simons_](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/206/392/) and the [school-search briefing](/posts/fourth-amendment-school-searches/). A device policy is not a substitute for analyzing the facts and applicable constitutional standard.

## Why It Matters to Your Rights & Wallet

Before using a school-issued device for personal messages or files, read the acceptable-use and monitoring policies and assume school administrators may have access described there. If a device is searched, note what officials said they were looking for, which accounts or files they accessed, and whether the search expanded. _Simons_ is a workplace case; it should not be used by itself to decide whether a particular student search was lawful.
