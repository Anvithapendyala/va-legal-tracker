## Status & Jurisdiction

Virginia's minimum wage appears in Va. Code § 40.1-28.10; employee definitions and exclusions are in § 40.1-28.9. As of November 23, 2026, the Virginia minimum wage is $12.77 per hour, or the higher federal minimum if applicable. The Code schedules a $13.75 state rate beginning January 1, 2027. Several definitions and exclusions also have future effective versions, so check the date that applies to the work performed.

## What Happened / The Legal Rule

Virginia's employee definition contains specific exceptions; it does not exempt every teen or every part-time worker. The current version of § 40.1-28.9 excludes, among others, people under 16, people under 18 employed by a parent or legal guardian, and certain full-time students under 18 who work no more than 20 hours a week. It also excludes qualifying workers at summer camps and people who are exempt from the federal minimum wage under the seasonal amusement-or-recreational-establishment provision. The statute has a separate future version beginning January 1, 2027; compare that text before assuming the current list continues unchanged.

Tipped employees are treated under separate wage-calculation rules. A restaurant cannot simply label a worker “tipped” to avoid wage duties: the statutory definition, actual tips, applicable federal rules, and any required shortfall payment must be considered. A young worker should compare hours worked, tips, deductions, and gross wages on each pay statement.

Read [Va. Code § 40.1-28.10](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title40.1/chapter3/section40.1-28.10/) and the effective versions of [§ 40.1-28.9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title40.1/chapter3/section40.1-28.9/). Federal wage law may provide separate protections even when a Virginia-law exclusion applies.

## Why It Matters to Your Rights & Wallet

Keep a personal record of shifts, breaks, tips, and pay. If you are under 18, do not assume that age alone means a lower wage applies; check the exact statutory exception and any federal rule that may cover the job. For a disputed paycheck, preserve schedules and pay stubs and ask the employer in writing how it calculated the hours and rate.
