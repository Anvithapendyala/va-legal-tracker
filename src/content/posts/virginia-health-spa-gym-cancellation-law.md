## Status & Jurisdiction

The Virginia Health Club Act applies to covered health-club contracts. The key cancellation provisions are Va. Code §§ 59.1-297 and 59.1-297.1. This briefing reflects the Code available on November 23, 2026; read the current statute and your signed agreement before acting.

## What Happened / The Legal Rule

A buyer may cancel a covered health-club contract without penalty within three business days after making it and receive a refund of amounts paid. The Act also provides cancellation rights if the facility relocates or closes without a comparable alternate facility within five driving miles, and if the buyer dies or becomes physically unable to use a substantial portion of the services for at least 30 consecutive days. A disability cancellation requires a signed statement from a doctor, physician assistant, or advanced practice registered nurse.

For these statutory cancellations, the buyer must give written notice by certified mail, return receipt requested, or personal delivery to the address in the contract. The refund is calculated under § 59.1-297.1 and generally must be issued within 30 days. That 30-day period is a refund deadline; it is not a universal 30-day cancellation window. Automatic-renewal deadlines and ordinary early-termination charges should be checked in the contract.

Read [Va. Code § 59.1-297](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title59.1/chapter24/section59.1-297/) and [§ 59.1-297.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title59.1/chapter24/section59.1-297.1/).

## Why It Matters to Your Rights & Wallet

Before joining, save a copy of the complete agreement and calendar any renewal or notice date. If a statutory reason to cancel applies, follow the required written-notice method and keep proof of delivery. A verbal conversation at the front desk may not satisfy the statute.
